# Zebra Eligibility & Packaging Requirements

## Purpose

This document contains the eligibility and packaging requirements for cases being considered for referral to the Zebra partner.

The requirements below are based on the current Zebra rules and guidance.

---

## Financial Eligibility

### Minimum DI

The minimum required disposable income (DI) is **£110**.

The client must repay over **£6,600 during the term**.

### Minimum Debt Level

The minimum debt level is **£7,000**.

### Minimum Income

The minimum client income is **£1,000**.

---

## Bank Statements

A minimum of **3 continuous full months** of bank statements is required.

Bank statements:

* Must show the client's name and address.
* Income and expenditure shown on the bank statements should correspond with the Income & Expenditure (I&E).
* Where figures do not correspond, the difference must be explained in the case notes.

---

## Income Evidence

Proof of all income is required.

Acceptable evidence includes:

* At least one wage slip dated within the last 3 months
* Benefit letter
* Bank statement
* Evidence of board
* CSA evidence
* Full Tax Return, not just the tax calculation

The minimum income requirement is **£1,000**.

### Partner Income

Proof of the partner's income is required.

### Housing Benefit Income

Evidence is required showing:

* The actual rental amount
* The benefit amount

Alternatively, confirmation that payments are up to date may be provided.

### Universal Credit

A breakdown of what is included within the Universal Credit payment is required.

### Universal Credit Advance

A Universal Credit Advance:

* Must be entered as a debt.
* Any deduction amount must be added back onto the income in the I&E.

---

## Gambling

**GAMSTOP is required for any case where the gambling amount exceeds the monthly IVA contribution.**

---

## Evidence of Outgoings

Evidence must support the costs entered into the I&E.

This includes, where applicable:

* Rent
* Mortgage
* Secured loan payments
* Childcare
* CSA

Where the evidence differs from the amount entered into the I&E, the difference must be explained in a case note.

### Rent / Board Evidence

Examples of acceptable evidence include:

* Bank transactions
* Tenancy agreement
* Three-way call confirming the rent amount and, where applicable, any arrears and repayment arrangement
* Email from the landlord or estate agent
* Where living with family, evidence that board is higher than £350

---

## Evidence of Debts

Where a debt does not appear on the Credit Search, proof of the debt must be held on file to confirm that the client remains liable for it.

This includes, but is not limited to:

* HMRC debts
* Council tax
* Payday loans
* Credit unions
* Student loans
* EDL
* Rent arrears
* CCJs
* Fines

---

## Childcare Vouchers

Where the client receives Tax-Free Childcare vouchers, evidence is required.

This should be provided by:

* Voucher evidence, or
* Payslip showing the payment

---

## Private Debt / Non-Common Creditors

Full contact details are required for private debts or non-common creditors.

Required details:

* Name
* Full address
* Email address
* Mobile number

---

## Council Tax Debt

All previous years' council tax debt must be included.

If the client's **Right to Pay has been revoked for the current year**, the current year's council tax debt must also be included.

### Council Tax and the I&E

The council tax expense should be listed as normal within the I&E.

The client's IVA payment will be:

**DI + council tax expense until April**

The payment will then reduce to the proposed DI.

### Council Tax Arrears

All previous years' council tax debts must be included.

The current year's council tax debt is dependent on the circumstances and will be dealt with at verification.

---

## Assets

All assets must be evidenced.

This includes assets such as:

* Property
* Vehicles
* Livestock
* Other relevant assets

The valuation obtained must be evidenced.

The client's intention regarding each asset must also be explained, for example:

* Retain
* Exclude
* Sell

---

## HP Agreements

A Credit Search can be used as evidence of a Hire Purchase (HP) agreement.

If a third party is paying the HP for the client, further evidence is required.

---

## HMRC Majority

Any **interlocking IVA with a joint HMRC majority debt** must be referred as **two sole IVAs**.

HMRC will reject interlocking IVAs where there is a joint debt.

---

## Student Income

Where a client has student income, at least **one third** of the student income should be offset for books and other applicable costs.

### WATCH Cases

If the case is on WATCH:

* Student loans must be fully offset.
* Bursary and grant income can be used as income.
* Any bursary or grant used as income must be noted.
* Proof must be attached.

---

## Guardianship / Foster Allowance

Guardianship and Foster Allowance is classed as income.

It **cannot be offset**.

---

## Vulnerable Clients

All vulnerable clients must have a note on the file detailing:

* The relevant conditions or circumstances
* Any possible implications for the application

Clients who have suffered domestic abuse, or any other circumstances deemed necessary, must be made aware that through an IVA their **name and address will appear on the Insolvency Register**.

---

## First Payment

The client must make their first payment in the **same calendar month as approval**.

If the first payment is made more than **6 weeks after the Approval Date**, the case will be in breach.

---

## Previous Addresses

A minimum of **5 years' address history** must be obtained.

The dates during which the client lived at each address must be recorded in the notes.

---

## Dependants / Children

Dates of birth are required for **all children**, including children who do not live at the client's property.

The percentage of time the client has the child must also be recorded, for example:

* 25%
* 50%
* 100%

---

## Social Fund / Budget Loans

Social Fund and Budget Loans **cannot be added as a debt**.

The following information is required:

* Amount outstanding
* Amount currently being deducted, if applicable

---

## Fines

Certain fines can be included.

Criminal fines cannot be included.

Examples of fines that can be included:

* TV Licence fine
* Speeding fine

For fines that are **not being included**, the following must be known for the I&E:

* Repayment amount
* Term of the repayment plan

---

## Previous Debt Solutions

Previous debt solutions must be checked.

Creditors need to be informed of previous debt solutions.

Previous debt solutions may also provide justification for proceeding with an IVA.

If a previous IVA was terminated, the termination documents are required.

---

## Budget Building

If any of the following are included in the I&E:

* Dentist
* Prescriptions
* Life Insurance
* Buildings & Contents Insurance

there must be detailed notes explaining what the costs are for.

If **all four** are included in the I&E, WATCH will request an uplift from one of the areas.

WATCH will not allow white goods insurance.

---

## Guarantor Debts

The client **must be advised that the guarantor will be chased for the debt**.

---

## Joint Debts

The client **must be advised that the other person will be chased for the debt**.

If the other person is the client's cohabiting partner, consideration must be given to whether money has been allowed in the I&E for the partner's debts.

---

## Partner Expense Allowance

The partner's allowance should be proportionate to the percentage of household income contributed by the partner.

Example:

If the partner has **50% of the household income**, the partner should be allocated **50% of the household DI**.

The WATCH calculator should be used.

---

## Dependants Turning 18 During the IVA

If a dependent turns 18 during the IVA and the client receives benefits that may stop or reduce as a result, this must be considered.

The client must be asked:

* What they plan to do when the dependent turns 18.
* How they propose to continue affording the IVA if their benefits stop or reduce.

---

## Rent Arrears

Rent arrears relating to the current property may be included in the IVA where the landlord or agent has confirmed that the current tenancy will not be affected.

If the rent arrears cannot be included in the IVA:

* Proof of the outstanding amount is required.
* Proof of the monthly repayment amount is required.
* A step-up will be implemented.

---

## Councils and Credit Unions with an AOE

Council and Credit Union debts with an Attachment of Earnings (AOE) are difficult to get approved.

This is because the creditor is receiving **100p in the £** through the AOE.

---

## Sole I&E for a Joint Household

A sole I&E for a joint household must only be used where the client:

* Is not married
* Has no dependants
* Has no joint assets

The full household costs must be established, including:

* Rent
* Council tax
* Utilities
* Other essential expenditure

It must be established what proportion of these costs the client actually pays and evidence should support this.

The client **cannot be recorded as paying 100% of essential household expenditure** where this does not reflect the circumstances.

The partner must be aware that they remain liable for joint bills.

---

## Disability Benefits

It must be disclosed who receives disability benefits.

### DLA

DLA must be **fully offset for children**.

### PIP

PIP must be **at least 50% offset**.

---

# General Evidence Principle

Where a Zebra requirement specifies that evidence is required, the evidence should be held on file.

Where the evidence does not correspond with the information recorded in the I&E, the discrepancy should be explained in the case notes where the rule specifically requires this.
