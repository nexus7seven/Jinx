# ZEBRA — Voting House Rules

Source workbook: Zebra Criteria (1).xlsx, sheet Watch.

## WATCH
- [Row 6] Must have 2 debts minimum, owed to 2 different creditors
- [Row 7] No recent spending within 3 months, except for essential goods (adult, work, school or children's clothing)
- [Row 8] No 3rd party income to sustain I&E
- [Row 10] Maximum car value is £8,999 if owned outright (If over, Watch will reject or add modification to sell)
- [Row 11] Maximum HP/PCP is £400 p/m, if over, downgrade modification will be applied. Client should be made aware from the outset
- [Row 12] If equity in vehicle, Watch will apply mod for client to sell vehicle or reject
- [Row 15] Board and lodging - maximum is £450 (anything over this amount would require 3 months historical evidence to verify it)
- [Row 16] Client must not be able to clear their debt level within 72 months, however an assumed 17% interest can be used in the calculation
- [Row 19] Average property valuation will be used, using two or three online appraisal sites (Zoopla, Mouseprice, other)
- [Row 20] Homeowner equity - must not exceed 100k (unless over 55 or have adaptations to property - will need evidence of adaptations)
- [Row 21] Will NOT accept owned outright cases
- [Row 22] Student loan income needs to be offset in full - cannot use a loan to repay unsecured borrowing - sustabibilty note also required for when income is lost
- [Row 23] Watch will abstain from voting if the client is aged over 80

## TIX
- [Row 27] Must have 2 debts minimum, owed to 2 different creditors unless direct negotiations attempted/arrangement failed
- [Row 28] Reject recent spending in last 6 months (Tesco/Shop Direct/Creation/Lendable)
- [Row 30] High equity in property will be accepted
- [Row 31] TIX will accept owned outright properties
- [Row 32] Maximum car value is £10,000 (If over, TIX will reject or add modification to sell)
- [Row 33] Maximum HP/PCP is £300 p/m, if over, downgrade or extension modification will be applied

## HMRC
- [Row 41] Evidence MUST be on file to confirm that client's Tax Returns are up to date - this should be proven using live chat/3way call
- [Row 42] £150 minimum DI
- [Row 44] BKY dividend MUST be lower than IVA
- [Row 45] Will reject if AOE in place
- [Row 46] Reject where the debt stems from historical unpaid self-employed tax and there is a record of poor compliance over multiple years.
