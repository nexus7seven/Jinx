# ZEBRA — Statutory Rules

Source workbook: Zebra Criteria (1).xlsx

- [Row 6] Minimum DI | £110 (must re-pay over £6600 during term) | 1st Payment Required | Client will be required to make their first payment is the same calendar month as the approval. Any later than 6 weeks after the Approval Date will result in the case being in breach.
- [Row 7] Minimum Debt Level | £7000 | Previous Addresses | We MUST obtain 5 years address history - we need dates of when the client was in each address - this should be left in the notes
- [Row 8] Bank Statements | 3 continuous full month as a minimum, Income and expenditure should correspond with the I & E or be explained in case notes (must show clients name and address) | Dependants / Children | We required dates of births of ALL children including those not living in the property with the client, as well as % of time with child i.e. 25%, 50% etc
- [Row 9] Universal Credit | Breakdown of the what is included is required | Universal Credit Advance | MUST be entered as a debt and any deduction amount added back on to the income on the I&E
- [Row 10] Gambling | GAMSTOP required for any cases where the amount exceeds the monthly IVA contribution. | Social Fund / Budget Loans | CANNOT be added in as a debt. We need how much outstanding, how much is being deducted if any.
- [Row 11] Proof of all income | At least 1 wage slip dates within the last 3 months/benefit letter/bank statement/evidence of board/CSA/Full Tax Return (not the calculation) £1000 minimum income.
- [Row 13] Partner's income | Proof of Partner income required
- [Row 19] Rent Arrears (current property) | This may be included in the IVA, as long as the landlord/agent has confirmed the current tenancy will not be effected.
- [Row 20] Assets (Property, Vehicles, Livestock etc) | All assets must be evidenced including the valuation obtained and the clients intention explained, eg retain/exclude/sell
- [Row 21] HMRC Majority | Any INTERLOCKING IVA with a JOINT HMRC MAJORITY debt must be referred as 2 SOLE IVA'S, HMRC will reject all interlocking IVA's where there is a joint debt
- [Row 22] Student Income | If the client has student income, at least one third of this should be offset for books etc ***IF WATCH ON CASE STUDENT LOANS MUST BE FULLY OFFSET - BURSARY AND GRANT CAN BE USED AS INCOME BUT NEEDS TO BE NOTED AND PROOF ATTACHED***
- [Row 23] Guardianship / Foster Allowance | This is classed as an income and cannot be offset | Disability Benefits | Must discolse who receives this. DLA needs to be fully offset for children and PIP must be minimum 50% offset
