# Zebra SIP Call Booking Process

Use this process when an agent asks how to book a SIP call with Zebra.

1. Push the lead from Lead2Case to Zebra HubSolv.
2. Fill out the handover sheet, then copy and paste the completed handover into the HubSolv notes.
3. Complete the I&E, Debts, Employment, Assets and Living tabs.
4. Make sure the case is assigned to Alex.
5. Leave the case status as "Not contacted".
6. Book the SIP call in the diary at: https://insolvencyguidancegroupsips.setmore.com/
7. Do not book the SIP call with Paul Peak Summers, John Bickerton or Kev Roberts.
8. After the booking is made, Alex should message the Zebra Group chat to notify them that there has been a booking.

When answering booking-process questions, preserve these operational steps and restrictions. Do not omit the assignment, status, excluded advisers, or Zebra Group notification steps.
