# Assure Criteria

## Scope

These rules apply only to Avondale cases intended for Assure. Avondale partner-level rules still apply unless this file contains a more specific Assure rule.

Creditor voting intentions, voting-house behaviour and fixed-fee voting data from the supplied workbook are intentionally excluded from this criteria codex and will be handled separately.

## Financial Eligibility

- Minimum DI: £110.
- The client must repay over £6,600 during the term.
- Minimum debt level: £7,000.
- Minimum client income: £1,000.

## Bank Statements

- Minimum 3 continuous full months.
- Must show the client's name and address.
- Income and expenditure should correspond with the I&E or discrepancies should be explained in case notes.

## Income Evidence

Proof of all income is required. Acceptable examples in the supplied criteria include:

- at least one wage slip dated within the last 3 months;
- benefit letter;
- bank statement;
- evidence of board;
- CSA evidence;
- full Tax Return, not just the tax calculation.

Proof of partner income is required where applicable.

## Universal Credit

- A breakdown of what is included within Universal Credit is required.
- A Universal Credit Advance must be entered as a debt.
- Any deduction amount for the UC Advance must be added back onto income in the I&E.

## Gambling

GAMSTOP is required where gambling exceeds the monthly IVA contribution.

## Evidence of Outgoings

Evidence must support applicable rent, mortgage, secured loan, childcare and CSA amounts. Where evidence differs from the I&E, the difference should be explained in a case note.

Rent/board evidence examples include bank transactions, tenancy agreement, a three-way call confirming rent and any arrears/arrangement, landlord/agent email, and evidence where family board exceeds £350.

## Evidence of Debts

Where a debt is not on the Credit Search, proof must be held to confirm liability. The supplied criteria specifically mention HMRC, council tax, payday loans, credit unions, student loans, EDL, rent arrears, CCJs and fines.

## Childcare Vouchers

Tax-Free Childcare vouchers require evidence via voucher evidence or a payslip showing the payment.

## Private / Non-Common Creditors

Full contact details are required: name, full address, email and mobile.

## Council Tax

- All previous years' council tax debt must be included.
- If Right to Pay has been revoked for the current year, the current year must also be included as a debt.
- The normal council tax expense remains in the I&E.
- The IVA payment is DI plus council tax expense until April, then reduces to the proposed DI.

## Assets

All assets must be evidenced, including valuation and the client's intention such as retain, exclude or sell.

## HP Agreements

A Credit Search can be used as evidence. If a third party pays the HP for the client, further evidence is required.

## HMRC Majority

Any interlocking IVA with a joint HMRC majority debt must be referred as two sole IVAs.

## Student Income

- At least one third should be offset for books and applicable costs.
- Where WATCH applies, student loans must be fully offset.
- Bursary and grant can be used as income but must be noted and evidenced.

## Foster / Guardianship Allowance

This is income and cannot be offset.

## Vulnerable Clients

A file note must detail the relevant condition/circumstances and possible implications. Clients affected by domestic abuse or other relevant circumstances must be made aware that their name and address appears on the Insolvency Register through an IVA.

## First Payment

The first payment is required in the same calendar month as approval. More than 6 weeks after the Approval Date places the case in breach.

## Previous Addresses

A minimum of 5 years' address history is required, including dates at each address, recorded in the notes.

## Dependants / Children

Dates of birth are required for all children, including those not living with the client. The percentage of time with each child must also be recorded.

## Social Fund / Budget Loans

These cannot be added as a debt. Record the amount outstanding and the deduction amount if applicable.

## Fines

Certain non-criminal fines can be included, with examples including TV Licence and speeding fines. Where a fine is not included, the repayment amount and repayment term must be known for the I&E.

## Previous Debt Solutions

Previous debt solutions must be checked and creditors informed. If a previous IVA terminated, termination documents are required.

## Budget Building

If Dentist, Prescriptions, Life Insurance or Buildings & Contents Insurance are included, detailed notes are required. If all four are included, WATCH may request an uplift from one area. White goods insurance is not allowed under the supplied WATCH rule.

## Guarantor Debts

The client must be advised that the guarantor will be chased.

## Joint Debts

The client must be advised that the other person will be chased. If that person is the cohabiting partner, consider whether the I&E allows money for the partner's debts.

## Partner Expense Allowance

The partner allowance should be proportionate to their percentage of household income. The supplied criteria refer to using the WATCH calculator.

## Dependants Turning 18 During IVA

Where benefits may stop or reduce when a dependant turns 18, establish what the client plans to do and how the IVA will remain affordable.

## Rent Arrears

Current-property rent arrears may be included where the landlord/agent confirms the tenancy will not be affected. If not included, evidence of the balance and monthly repayment is required and a step-up is implemented.

## Councils / Credit Unions with AOE

These are difficult to approve because the creditor is receiving 100p in the £ through the Attachment of Earnings.

## Sole I&E for Joint Household

Only where the client is not married, has no dependants and has no joint assets. Establish full household costs and the client's genuine share. The client cannot be shown as paying 100% of essential household expenditure where that does not reflect reality. The partner must be aware they remain liable for joint bills.

## Disability Benefits

- Disclose who receives the benefit.
- Children's DLA must be fully offset.
- PIP must be at least 50% offset.

## Source Handling
