# Lawson Fox Self-Employed Criteria

## Scope

These rules apply only when assessing a self-employed or director case for Lawson Fox through Avondale.

The spreadsheet's cashflow/calculation tabs are intentionally excluded at this stage. This file contains criteria and evidence requirements only.

## Questions / Facts to Establish

For all self-employed clients establish:

- whether all tax returns are up to date;
- what business expenses are for, and ensure they are reflected in the cashflow evidence;
- whether the client is in CIS;
- whether the client pays tax on self-employment and, if not, why;
- whether there are outstanding HMRC debts.

For a director of a limited company also establish:

- whether they are a salaried director;
- whether they owe money to the company;
- whether the company owes money to them;
- whether an IVA affects their directorship;
- whether they receive dividends;
- whether the company is in a position to pay those dividends;
- whether tax returns are up to date and dated within the last 6 months;
- if not, Management Accounts are required and cannot be produced merely from bank statements.

## Eligibility / Core Criteria

- If turnover is £85,000 or more, the supplied criteria state VAT registration would be required and a VAT debt would be preferential; in that circumstance the case is not accepted under this sheet.
- The business must be making a profit.
- The client must not have employees.
- The client must have traded for at least 6 months.
- The business must not be a partnership.
- Tax Returns must be up to date.
- Tax Returns for previous years must have been submitted to HMRC.

## Evidence / Trading History

If the Tax Return does not accurately reflect current income, use 6 months' bank statements to evidence the cashflow.

### Trading less than 1 year

- Minimum 6 months trading.
- 6 months bank statements.
- Invoices.
- Completed income breakdown spreadsheet showing business incomings and outgoings and how income has been calculated.
- Previous-year Tax Return still required where applicable.

### Trading over 1 year

- Latest Tax Return.
- 3 months business bank statements.
- If no business account exists, 3 months personal bank statements plus completed income breakdown spreadsheet.

## HMRC / Evidence

- Call recording or live chat is required to confirm the full HMRC balance going back 10 years.
- If the client is a director, the relevant evidence requirements apply to both the client and the business.
- Evidence of expenditure is required.
- Any forecast increase in trading should be evidenced on bank statements through increased income or reduced expenditure.
- Handwritten invoices or self-typed expenses cannot be used as evidence under the supplied criteria.
