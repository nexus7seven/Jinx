# LAWSON FOX — Statutory Rules

Source workbook: Avondale - Lawson Fox Criteria(1).xlsx

- [Row 6] Minimum DI | 110 Per case, we do not run joint IVAs so partners should be referred as 2 separate cases with each others IVA payment included in the I&E.
- [Row 7] Minimum Debt Level | 7500
- [Row 8] Bank Statements | 3 continuous full month as a minimum, Income and expenditure should correspond with the I & E or be explained in case notes (must show clients name and address)
- [Row 9] Universal Credit | Breakdown of the what is included is required | Universal Credit Advance | MUST be entered as a debt and any deduction amount added back on to the income on the I&E
- [Row 10] Gambling | GAMSTOP required for any cases where the amount exceeds the monthly IVA contribution
- [Row 11] Proof of all income | At least 1 wage slip dates within the last 3 months/benefit letter/bank statement/evidence of board/CSA/Full Tax Return (not the calculation) £1000 minimum income.
- [Row 13] Partner's income & Children | Partner Name and email required for all cases with a partner. Dates of birth for all children including any they pay child support for.
- [Row 19] Rent Arrears (current property) | Whe do not propose current rent arrears they should have this built inot the I&E with a a confirmed payment plan for the arrears
- [Row 20] Assets (Property, Vehicles, Livestock etc) | All assets must be evidenced including the valuation obtained and the clients intention explained, eg retain/exclude/sell
- [Row 21] HMRC Majority | Any INTERLOCKING IVA with a JOINT HMRC MAJORITY debt must be referred as 2 SOLE IVA'S, HMRC will reject all interlocking IVA's where there is a joint debt
- [Row 23] Guardianship / Foster Allowance | This is classed as an income and cannot be offset | Disability Benefits | Must discolse who receives this. DLA needs to be fully offset for children and PIP must be minimum 50% offset
