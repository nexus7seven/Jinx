# TIG Criteria

## Scope

These rules apply only to Avondale cases intended for TIG. Avondale partner-level rules still apply unless this file contains a more specific TIG rule.

This file intentionally excludes creditor-specific voting intentions, voting-house behaviour, fixed-fee outcomes and creditor accept/reject predictions from the supplied workbook. Those will be handled by a separate voting-intentions capability.

## Financial Eligibility

- Minimum debt level: £6,000.
- Minimum DI: £100.
- Financial Statement must be within SFS guidelines.
- Avondale's partner-level I&E rule still requires relevant SFS-controlled sections to be built at 65% of the applicable SFS maximum unless a later TIG-specific rule supersedes it.
- DLA/PIP must be offset.

## Income Evidence

- One full month wage slip is required for each wage used in the I&E, including partner wages where applicable.
- Wage evidence should be dated within the last 3 months.
- If unavailable in exceptional circumstances, portal or similar evidence may be used with manager sign-off.
- Ensure the tax code is correct.
- Benefit evidence must be from the current financial year or shown on a recent bank statement in the current financial year.
- Universal Credit requires the full journal dated within the last 3 months.

## Self-Employed / CIS

- Standard self-employed evidence: Tax Return plus 3 months banking.
- Newly self-employed clients must have been in the job for a minimum of 3 months, with confirmation of hours and pay/income information.
- CIS: minimum one month's invoice showing the 20% deduction.

These are assessment/evidence criteria only. No self-employed cashflow calculator from the workbook has been imported.

## Bank Statements

- Bank statements must be dated within the last 3 months.
- One full month is required for every account, including savings accounts.
- It must be clear who owns each account.
- Direct debits, standing orders and regular transactions must be explained where they are not reflected in the I&E or where the amount differs.

## Gambling

- Gambling must be under £1,000 under the supplied TIG criteria.
- GAMSTOP is required if gambling exceeds £200.

## Proof of Debts

Proof is required for debts used to verify the case. The supplied criteria allow a combination of:

- Credit Report;
- creditor letters dated within the last 6 weeks, including reference numbers;
- debt-collector letters dated within the last 6 weeks showing original creditor information, reference numbers and original creditor names;
- three-way calls with creditors/debt collectors confirming reference number and full balance including interest;
- CCJ details, including original creditor where the client does not know it.

Debts under £1,000 may be accepted verbally where proof cannot be obtained, unless that debt is needed to meet the debt-level requirement.

Where Shop Direct appears, the supplied criteria require a credit report showing granular details of recent spending.

## Third-Party Evidence / Contributions

Third-party letters must be signed and dated, show the third party's address and include either a mobile number or email address.

For any third-party contribution, establish how long it will last.

## Previous IVA

Where the client had a previous IVA, establish:

- when it failed;
- why it failed;
- what has changed since the previous proposal, including DI/debt-level changes;
- the termination report is required.

## HMRC / Council / Creditor-Specific Rules

The supplied TIG sheet also contains a number of creditor-specific statements such as HMRC, council-majority, Link, Shop Direct and Creation accept/reject behaviour. These have deliberately not been imported into this criteria file because they are voting intentions and will be modelled separately.

## Source Handling

Source: supplied Avondale - TIG criteria workbook, TIG Criteria sheet. Voting-intention material was separated out rather than treated as general TIG eligibility knowledge.
